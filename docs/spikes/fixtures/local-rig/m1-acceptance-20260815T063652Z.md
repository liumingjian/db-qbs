## Review conclusions (2026-08-15, first real-rig run)

1. **Push/cursor > 50% on narrow-100k (63.6%) and kill-direct (52.7%) — the ADR-0007 review
   line is formally triggered.** Reviewed and the serial fetch+push decision stands: the
   ratio is high only because total cursor lifetime is tiny (narrow 1.8 s, kill 1.0 s,
   wide 25.0 s for 100k rows). ADR-0007's risk model is ORA-01555 on cursors held for
   minutes to hours; at seconds of lifetime the proportion is not a signal. Revisit only
   if real workloads push cursor lifetime into minutes.
2. **Commit duration vs the 30-minute read timeout (ADR-0010 §4/§10):** wide commit
   10.4 s (COUNT 145 ms), narrow 0.35 s, kill-rerun 0.47 s. Three orders of magnitude of
   headroom; the guessed 30-minute value needs no adjustment.
3. **Batch bytes vs the 16 MiB budget (ADR-0011 §6):** wide max 16,776,616 bytes stays
   under the 16,777,216-byte budget, and the byte cutter fired before the 5000-row cutter
   (21 batches of 4137–4795 rows). Narrow batches are row-capped (20×5000, ~0.22 MB).
   No PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE anywhere — the budget logic works as specified.
